   ⑧ transition: filter → removed everywhere except skeleton

   KEPT FOR BEAUTY:
   ✓ Static radial-gradient backgrounds (zero cost)
   ✓ transform transitions on buttons (60fps compositor)
   ✓ opacity transitions on cards (60fps compositor)
