ANALYTICS ARCHITECTURE RULES

Separation of concerns
- Search/list/detail operational flow must stay independent from heavyweight analytics reads.
- Analytics datasets are explicit, opt-in reads and refresh triggers.

Analytics read surfaces
- SimpleAnalyticalSet: lightweight analytical summary read.
- WorkflowAnalyticsBreakdownSet: heavyweight breakdown read.
- AnalyticsRefreshStateSet: refresh state read/progress projection.
- AnalyticsRefreshTrigger: explicit backend refresh command.

Frontend rules
- Search must not implicitly trigger heavyweight analytics loads.
- Analytics dialogs/pages may request analytics datasets explicitly when entered.
- UI meaning colors must represent actual status/risk semantics, not arbitrary filter context.

Backend rules
- Analytics refresh remains command-driven.
- Analytics projections are read models; they do not redefine operational search/detail contracts.
